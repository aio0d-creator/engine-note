Hold REV to rev when the car is stopped. On the move, the sound follows your GPS speed.

Garage

Connect your phone to the car by Bluetooth so the sound plays through the car speakers, and keep the screen on. Each car's sound is generated live to resemble its engine. For the real sound, load a recording of that car (a steady idle or constant-rev clip works best) and set the rpm it was recorded at. Car names only describe the engine; this site isn't connected to any car maker. Keep the volume low enough to hear sirens and horns, and set everything up before you drive.
